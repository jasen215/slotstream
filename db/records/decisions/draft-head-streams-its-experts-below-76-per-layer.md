Carlos approved landing the tested decode changes on September 24, 2026: "yes, land them in Slotstream". The proposal he accepted named the streamed draft head and a head floor near 25 experts per layer; the measured floor is 28.

**Decision.** The planner places the draft head's 512 routed experts. On a cache that still reaches 76 experts per layer after the resident 1.6 GB charge they stay resident, exactly as before: 32 GB Macs and larger at their automatic targets. Below that they stream from `mtp.safetensors` through a 64-expert CLOCK cache, charged 0.389 GB: the rest of the head, 64 cached and 10 scratch records, and the resident charge's allowance. Automatic mode enables a streamed head where the cache after its charge reaches 28 experts per layer, and runs the decode lookahead with it from the same floor. `--mtp on` streams below 76 and keeps resident at or above. `SLOTSTREAM_MTP_EXPERTS=resident|streamed` forces one placement for comparisons. A loaded engine's governor and vision re-plans keep its placement and charge. The former automatic floor of 76 ([[records/decisions/draft-head-auto-floor-76-per-layer]]) now marks where the experts stay resident. The automatic context window keeps the placement of the 32,768-token plan: a larger window that would move a resident head's experts to streaming is declined, because the window's time estimate does not price the head's reads, so every Mac tier keeps its automatic window. Asked for explicitly, such a window keeps the head: `--max-context 65536` on a 32 GB Mac now streams the head's experts instead of turning the head off. A forced head fits from an 8.5 GB target, the 8.1 GB minimum plus the streamed charge.

**Evidence.** [[records/measurements/decode-perf-2026-09-24]], environment-guarded prototype with the keepalive and direct demand reads on both arms: at a 12 GB target, the streamed head with the lookahead decoded 1.23x faster than plain decode with the lookahead over three swap-free pairs (1.21x over eight, 8 of 8 above 1), with 28.4 experts per layer after the head's charge; the resident head tied plain decode there (1.010). At 10 GB, 17.4 per layer, the streamed head tied (0.988 over eight, 1.051 over three). At 16 GB the resident head already won by 1.32x over five swap-free pairs and the streamed head was 1.06x faster again over eight pairs. At 22 GB streamed and resident heads tied (1.008 over eight, 0.990 over four), which is why larger caches keep the resident head. The streamed head's cache hit 47% at depth 2 and its reads took 1.2 to 1.8 ms per output token. The landed build confirmed it at 12 GB: automatic mode, the streamed head with the lookahead at 25.5 experts per layer, against `--mtp off`, plain decode with the lookahead at 28.2, decoded 1.205x faster over eight pairs, all eight above 1 (1.093 to 1.329), and 1.189x over the six without swap-outs. Every pair recorded swap-ins, so this confirms the direction rather than a timing claim ([[sources/runs/2026/09/2026-09-24-draft-stream-landed]]).

**Safety.** Rows are served one at a time. A slot's bytes change only after the previous draft row's routing was read back, which required that row's gathers to finish; the verify pass never reads the head's slots, and consumption runs only the head's attention, so a streamed head never routes rows whose output nothing reads. A victim slot is unmapped before its bytes change, and a failed read leaves its slots empty and ends the request with an error. `draft-stream-check` compares streamed and resident heads with the prompt pass pinned and injects a read failure.

**Scope.** One M5 Pro 48 GB with its internal SSD, four prompts of 192 tokens, two drafts. Targets between the measured 12 and 22 GB interpolate between measured wins. A slower SSD makes the head's reads cost more; `SLOTSTREAM_MTP_EXPERTS=resident` restores the resident head and its floor.
